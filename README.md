# About

Luke Badgerow is a Principal Cloud Engineer, leading cloud infrastructure modernization, DevOps transformation, and driving efficiency and safety of Agentic delivery across engineering organizations

With 23 years of experience spanning full-stack application development, enterprise architecture, and large-scale cloud migrations, Luke brings a practitioner's perspective to AI-assisted development — grounded in the reality of regulated, enterprise environments where "move fast" has meaningful consequences. He has led DevOps and cloud transformation engagements across banking, healthcare, logistics, manufacturing, and retail, and has spent the last several years building the tooling, pipelines, and platform patterns that make AI augmentation practical at scale.

Luke's current focus is on how agentic AI changes the day-to-day work of platform and application engineering teams — not just as a productivity multiplier, but as a forcing function for better software design, clearer documentation, and more intentional automation. His work sits at the intersection of infrastructure-as-code, CI/CD, and AI-assisted developer workflows.
